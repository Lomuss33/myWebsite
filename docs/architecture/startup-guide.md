# Startup navigation guide

Feature ID: `startup-navigation-guide`. Search names: **Explore the edges**, **Explore the sidebar**, **Look around** (previous copy), Explore the site, menu on the left, edge guide, navigation hint, spotlight, startup overlay, dimmed Home, onboarding lighting, OpenDyslexic guide font, dyslexia-friendly captions, Entdecke die Ränder, Istraži rubove, Kenarları keşfet.

Verified: 2026-10-07 against controller input/timer logic, current styles, locally hosted fonts, caption resize observation and live layout/text probes in EN/DE/HR/TR and both themes. Current scope is detailed below. Earlier 2026-10-06 Chromium samples from 280×653 to 3440×1440 observed persistent display, idle replay, keyboard/native mobile/nested desktop scroll dismissal, interrupted entrance, live reduced motion, rotation and app pause/resume. Those samples do not certify the revised font or reminder timing. Physical Android suspension, Safari and Firefox behavior remain unverified.

Mobile uses one continuous, softly curved shade behind all three text groups, with its feather flowing beneath the navigation bars. It has no separate caption backings. Desktop keeps compact feathered backing and one local drop shadow per text group. Both share the main veil's RGB colour: black in dark mode and pale blue in light mode. OpenDyslexic regular and bold are hosted locally and apply to the guide in both themes. The main heading uses pure white in dark mode and pure black in light mode; supporting words use slightly greyed slate/navy without a pale glyph halo. Language actions have a separate rose accent, while light/dark words use amber/violet independently in all four translations. Navigation, tools and résumé actions use matching cyan, violet and gold accents, luminous in dark mode and deeper in light mode. All four languages explain navigation and adjustments with separate instruction rows, small functional icons and bold actions. Mobile centers each row, with three actions above and two navigation instructions below. Desktop has three left-aligned rail hints and a right-aligned headline above the page hint with a bounded right inset.

Route: `#about` (Home). This is visual guidance for the existing navigation, not a modal or an interactive control.

The desktop page veil uses at most 80% opacity in both themes, with a 24% transition stop around the clear spotlight. The shade/rail-hint layer is masked to at most 15% opacity over the measured left rail, including hint-shadow overlap. The mask stays faint through 16px beyond the rail for the toggle, then returns to full strength over 40px. Rail hints start beyond that fade; the separate headline keeps readable ink at a 24–96px right inset. Idle spotlight motion keeps opacity steady rather than pulsing the guide. Its clear area and movement keep their existing geometry. Mobile uses an 80% source opacity in both themes for its single blurred silhouette; the feather naturally lowers opacity near navigation. Separate accent-light sweeps follow the navigation controls without animating the main shade or text.

## Accepted behavior

- Show the guide at most four times per website load, including its initial appearance. Keep each visible guide until interaction, including the fourth; do not add an expiry timer. The in-memory budget survives leaving/returning to Home and controller recreation. Refreshing/reloading the website resets it; no local/session storage acknowledgement is written.
- Preserve the existing initial delay (1.1 seconds after readiness). On Home, clicks/taps, key presses and focus defer the reminder for 10 seconds; swipes, wheel input and scrolling defer it for 5 seconds after the last interaction. A swipe's movement replaces its initial touch's 10-second delay with 5 seconds. Add 5 seconds per previous appearance in the current website load: the first return waits 15 seconds after a press or 10 seconds after a swipe; the next waits 20/15 seconds. Before any appearance, the base delays remain 10/5 seconds. The initial 1.1-second entrance is unchanged. Count an overlay once when its entrance starts painting, including an entrance dismissed midway; resize/font/theme reconciliation does not count again. The fourth entrance clears pending reminder timers, and no initial/replay path schedules another appearance until reload. The dismissal fade consumes part of the delay, rather than starting another full delay afterward. Ordinary page animations and unrelated DOM changes do not reset it.
- Preserve pointer-movement, touch, click, keyboard, focus and scroll dismissal, including a nested page scroller. On Home, actual mouse/pen movement while hidden pushes replay to at least the 5-second base plus the current session increment after the last movement, while respecting any later deadline already established by a click/key press. Continuous movement therefore keeps the guide hidden; early movement cannot shorten the 10-second click/key pause. Identical coordinates from compatibility events do not reset inactivity. Keep the original initial movement threshold. Captured passive input also reaches the guide when a descendant stops bubbling; navigation and page controls remain usable through the overlay. Reminders run only on Home.
- On mobile, highlight the whole visible profile header/top navigation and the bottom navigation bar with softer edge areas. Use one broad, blurred silhouette spanning the measured content gap, with softly curved ends and no separate caption panels or full-width gradient seams. Its solid source overlaps each inner navigation edge by 60% of the blur radius; its feather extends farther underneath the bars. Blur scales with the smaller of viewport width and content-gap height, normally between 24px and 96px and capped at one quarter of the gap on short screens. Extend the shape horizontally by three blur radii per side so no panel edge appears; clip only at the overlay viewport. Caption placement remains independent of the paint. Retain this unified composition on large portrait screens rather than creating three isolated shadows.
- On desktop, retain the travelling sidebar spotlight and subsequent ambient motion with a strong but translucent page veil and an at least 85% transparent rail shade. Measure the rail independently of the spotlight's moving circle; mask the shade and rail hints there, with a short outward fade. The central headline and accent cues are separate. Keep ambient opacity at 1 rather than modulating it with the spotlight's radius, so the shade and caption do not pulse. Normal guide entrance/dismissal still uses its existing fade. Reduced-motion preference makes the lighting still; it does not shorten the guide's lifetime. Preference changes take effect while the guide is visible.
- Add a restrained light sweep down the whole measured desktop rail, including profile, links and tools. On mobile, sweep across the whole measured header/top-menu area and then the bottom navigation, half a cycle apart, in opposite directions. Use a faint wash and 2px edge glint, with cyan in dark mode and blue in light mode. Clip effects to the measured controls and use only transform/opacity during animation. Sweep spans scale with the travel distance and cap at 200px; larger screens get slower passes (desktop 6.4–10 seconds, mobile 7.2–11.2 seconds). Start after the guide entrance, including reminders; pause immediately on dismissal and remove with the guide. Reduced motion uses a still, faint edge and no moving wash. These cues do not change layout, caption opacity, reminder timing or the main shade.
- Dark mode uses a strong black veil; light mode uses a soft blue veil with deep navy text for both headings and instructions. All shading derives from `--startup-guide-veil-rgb`. Mobile's single silhouette shields the copy uniformly while keeping all lettering above its blur; caption wrappers have no filter, transform or backing pseudo-element. Desktop retains its opaque caption backing and stronger local drop shadow. Light mode has no glyph shadow, preventing a pale halo from softening dark letter edges. Keep all paint outside layout, with no visible panel edge, added content spacing or different tint/white halo.
- Recompute geometry for viewport/orientation, navigation size, font, layout, theme and language changes. Keep captions inside the visible viewport. On mobile, let each hint group use the available width up to 42rem; center individual icon/phrase rows and their wrapped copy. Reduce vertical insets before omitting a hint, keeping the shade's feather independent. Fit the headline between the groups; retain the lower navigation hint first when space runs out. On desktop, right-align the headline with a 24–96px right inset and normally place it 32–96px above vertical center. Limit its width to the available content beyond the rail fade. Keep it above the page hint with a measured gap. Align the three left-aligned hints with the visible résumé control, page-link group and settings group; omit a hint only when its control is absent. On a tight desktop, reserve space between hints for the headline; if it cannot fit, omit the generic headline and restore it when space returns.
- Stop animations and pending waits when dismissed, destroyed, hidden or paused. Reconcile Home and its navigation on return. Leaving Home removes the guide.
- Use a restrained text hierarchy without visible boxes, pills, borders, status dots or broad glow clouds. Desktop groups have compact feathered backing and one additive drop shadow; mobile groups share the continuous shade. Give each action its own instruction row with a small functional symbol; emphasise actions with bold type and restrained theme-aware colours: teal navigation, rose language, amber light mode/résumé and violet dark mode/tools. The mobile upper hint explains language, light/dark mode and viewing/downloading the résumé by the profile; the lower hint explains choosing a section below and finding additional pages in its top menu. Natural wrapping is allowed on tiny screens rather than forcing a single physical line. Do not imply that Home's hidden page submenu is visible: extra page menus appear for categories with multiple pages. Keep the central headline without a redundant subtitle or repeated destination names. Desktop has a résumé hint at the top, a page-links hint in the middle and theme/language instructions at the bottom. Explicitly use locally hosted OpenDyslexic in both themes, with actual 400/700 faces, comfortable line spacing and no condensed tracking; other site typography stays independent. EN/DE/HR/TR remain supported. The overlay stays `aria-hidden` and never takes focus; separate screen-reader/help UI remains an unimplemented proposal.

## Ownership and dependencies

Visible modal dialogs suppress the guide immediately, including portals mounted outside the React root. Hidden dialogs do not block reminders. Focused inputs, selects, textareas and all editable descendants (including plaintext-only editors) suppress reminders. Leaving Home releases the navigation size observations as well as removing the overlay. On a cramped mobile screen, omit the upper hint first, then the generic headline before dropping the lower navigation instructions; restore omitted groups when room returns. Never force lettering over navigation to fit it.

Read the controller first for timing or interaction bugs. Read the styles first for visual adjustments, then check the measured geometry in the controller.

The guide observes target sizes, the headline and all three hint sizes, and relevant navigation/Home mutations. Caption resize observation handles wrapping changes when either font weight finishes loading. Desktop additionally measures the profile résumé toggle or short-rail résumé band, page-link shell and tools group. It caches measured geometry for ambient animation frames. `startup-guide-paint` groups the shade and contextual hints; the headline and three absolute navigation cues sit outside its desktop rail mask. Cue dimensions and sweep lengths/durations update during geometry reconciliation; CSS handles their motion without new frame-by-frame measurements or JS animation timers. `data-guiding` starts/pauses their motion with guide visibility. Typed text updates must not reset reminder timers. Target selector changes in navigation components must also update the controller.

Caption headings are plain strings. `desktopTop`, `desktopMiddle`, `desktopBottom`, `top` and `bottom` are arrays of `{icon?, text, tone?, accents?}` rows; `desktopDetail` and `mobileDetail` stay empty. In row text, `**action**` marks bold accent text. Icon keys are limited to `left`, `down`, `up`, `settings`, `language`, `theme` and `resume`. A row without an icon supplies a short lead. Tones are `navigation` (default), `tools`, `download`, `language`, `light` and `dark`; `accents` optionally assigns tones to successive emphasised phrases. Each explicit tone resolves independently, so a navigation action inside a tools row keeps its navigation colour. Ordinary words always use the guide's body ink, headings their own ink, and icons match their row's action accent. Both themes share these roles on desktop and mobile; palette values live in `_page.scss`. Keep all four locales aligned; translations are never interpreted as HTML.

Use the [targeted validation guidance](../guides/validation.md). These checks describe what to inspect after an edit; they do not claim that every device was tested.

1. Open Home without interacting. Wait through loading and the initial delay; leave it idle beyond the entrance animation. It must stay visible until interaction. After the initial appearance, confirm a click/tap delays the first return for 15 seconds and a swipe/scroll for 10 seconds; confirm the next appearance adds another 5 seconds to each delay. Keep moving the mouse beyond that deadline: the guide must stay hidden and return after the base movement delay plus the current load's increment. Early movement must not shorten the click's 10-second pause. Typing animations must not restart timers. After the fourth appearance, dismiss it and confirm no fifth reminder, including after leaving/returning to Home. Reload to restore the initial appearance and a fresh four-show budget. Interrupting an entrance still consumes one appearance.
2. Sample a tiny phone (280×653), ordinary phone (390×844), tablet (768×1024), large portrait mobile layout (1440×2560), desktop (1366×768) and ultrawide (3440×1440). In both themes, check readable navigation, gradual transitions, readable copy and caption bounds. Check every ordinary and emphasised word, punctuation and icon after a theme switch; heading/body ink and all action tones must update together without leftover colours. Mobile must have one continuous shade with no isolated caption patches, straight navigation seams or added horizontal scrolling; its geometry must stay nonnegative on short screens. Sample all four languages.
3. Resize/rotate while visible. Confirm current layout mode, target geometry and captions update together. On desktop, check extended and short rails: all navigation stays almost unshaded, the darker page veil remains, and rail hints fit beyond the short fade. Check top résumé, middle pages and bottom settings hints; the headline stays right-aligned with its inset and above the page hint without overlap. The glint must sweep the entire rail. On mobile, check centered instruction rows, including wrapped translations, and alternating top/bottom glints covering the profile header; effects must stay inside measured controls. Watch through motion: the shade and caption opacity stay steady. Check the short desktop heading omission and its restoration. Enable reduced motion while visible: use still edges and stop the spotlight while keeping the guide visible. Dismiss and wait for replay; cue motion must restart with the new guide.
4. Dismiss using mouse/pen movement, Escape/Tab, clicks/taps and the inner Home scroller. Check a tap versus touch-start followed by a swipe, and input on a child that stops bubbling. Interrupt the entrance animation, leave Home or pause/resume the app; an obsolete animation must not revive removed markup or suppress later reminders. Other pages must never show the guide.
5. Check that ordinary navigation remains clickable and focusable, dialogs/editable controls do not receive a new guide, and animations perform no per-frame target/label layout measurements.
