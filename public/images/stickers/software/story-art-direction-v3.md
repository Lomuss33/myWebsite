# Software sticker artwork, revision 3

The user rejected revision 2 because it retained the drawings and mainly changed colour. This revision creates all 14 images anew with built-in imagegen. Previous PNGs are historical references only, not edit inputs. The scene briefs are in `prompts-story-v3.json`.

## Visual contract

- New subject arrangements, gestures and storytelling for every sticker, with one clear dominant shape and meaningful secondary detail.
- Professional 2D editorial illustration: differentiated character, architectural, technical, typographic and layered digital approaches. No uniform neon icon treatment.
- Medium-value opaque surfaces, dark graphite contours and selective narrow cool silver-blue rim details. The same PNG must remain legible against the supplied pale blush/peach and dark navy/rust themes.
- Local teal, coral, berry, cobalt, plum and cool slate accents vary with the story. Neon highlights never substitute for silhouette contrast.
- No cream/ivory paper carrier, distressed grain, thick white die-cut band, yellow/gold palette, diffuse glow cloud or fake transparency background.
- Square transparent master with connected scene occupying about 88–92% of the canvas, safe margins, intentional alpha gaps and strong readability at 80–104px.
- Keep all accepted positions, rotations, sizes, section-level layering and zero layout spacing.
